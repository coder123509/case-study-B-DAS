## Volume
- Largest time-series table (the one the brief's 100,000-row rule applies to): **1,271,160 rows** of 15-minute readings (5 stations x 12 parameters x 12 months), 6.8 MB gzipped, 159 MB as plain text and **771 MB in pandas memory** (text columns are expensive).
- That is above Excel's 1,048,576-row sheet limit, so the raw table cannot be opened in a spreadsheet at all.
- After keeping 4 pollutants and the study window it is 488,215 readings; after resampling, 43,800 station-hours.
- The static GTFS timetable is even larger (~2.1 M stop_times rows, ~130 MB unzipped), so we read only 2 of its columns and filter to our 351 stops straight away.

## Velocity
- Each station reports every 15 minutes: across 5 stations that is ~144 readings per hour (~3,464 per day).
- But velocity on paper is not velocity in practice: pm readings repeat the previous value 75% of the time (runs of 4). The gases (NO2, CO) really do change every 15 minutes.
- Weather arrives hourly and in UTC; AQ arrives in IST quarter-hours. Lining up 'the same hour' (the +05:30 half-hour offset) was the hardest time problem - see the slot rule in `src/utils.py`.

## Variety
- 7 sources, 5 formats: CSV, JSON (two very different shapes - column arrays vs nested elements), a ZIP of relational text files, a PDF table and free-text headlines; plus one calendar typed by hand.
- Three kinds of keys had to be reconciled: time (IST vs UTC, ISO strings vs compact strings), space (lat/lon distances between stations, stops, footpaths and flood points) and identity (OpenAQ location ids vs station names with suffixes, GTFS stop ids).

## Which V dominated, and what it changed
- **Volume + velocity** drove the tools: the 1.27 M-row, 15-minute table is too big for a spreadsheet and slow to iterate on in pandas memory, so cleaning and resampling are also implemented in **PySpark** (`spark/spark_resample_join.py`), and only the 8,760-row hourly city series goes into Excel.
- **Variety** cost the most *time*: every source needed its own parser (JSON flattening, PDF text layout, OSM geometry, GTFS joins) before anything could be joined, and the join key had to be designed (hourly slot + distance rules).
